Expert Knowledge

Green Thumb Care Guides

Lighting Mastery

Understand indirect sun angles. Place tropical foliage near east or south-facing windows with sheer curtains for optimal chlorophyll growth.

Hydration & Soil

Avoid root rot by checking soil moisture 2 inches deep. Water thoroughly only when dry, using organic compost-enriched potting mix.

Pest Prevention

Wipe large leaves monthly with organic neem oil emulsion to maintain natural shine and protect against microscopic spider mites.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How are plants packaged for shipping?
Every plant is secured with biodegradable padding, a moisture-locked soil collar, and a breathable box designed to prevent shifting or leaf damage in transit.
What if my plant arrives damaged?
We offer a 30-day healthy-arrival guarantee. Contact us with a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or refund.
Can I pause or cancel my subscription box?
Yes — the Seasonal Greenery Club can be paused, skipped, or cancelled anytime from your account with no cancellation fees.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we ship within the country only, to keep transit times short and plants healthy. International shipping is on our roadmap.
Are the pots included in the price?
Plants ship in a standard nursery pot by default. You can upgrade to a terracotta or ceramic planter at checkout for an additional cost.
